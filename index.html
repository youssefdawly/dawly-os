Protocols — run these, don't improvise
🚨 Overwhelm Protocol
Triggered the second you notice freeze or the urge to escape into the phone.

1. Stand up. Physically. Walk to another room.
2. 3 slow breaths (4s in, 6s out) — this settles the gut too.
3. Ask ONE question: "What is the smallest next physical action?" (open the CAD file, write one sentence, send one message)
4. Do it for 10 minutes only. Permission to stop after. You almost never will.
5. Phone goes to the other room BEFORE step 4, not after.
🔄 Reset Protocol (after 2+ missed days)
The old you abandoned systems here. The new you runs this instead:

1. No guilt audit. Don't review what you missed. Delete the mental debt.
2. Declare a Minimum Day: one 30-min deep work block + 5 outreach + sleep on time. That's a full win.
3. Two Minimum Days in a row → resume normal days.
4. Write one line in the Wins Ledger: "Came back on [date]." Coming back IS the elite skill.
🧠 Deep Work SOP
1. Phone in another room (non-negotiable — this is 80% of the system).
2. One tab, one file, one task written on paper before starting.
3. 50 min on / 10 min off. Off means stand, water, look far away — not screens.
4. Log the block in the Daily Log immediately — the streak is the reward.
5. Sharpest hours are your evening — guard 6 PM–10 PM like tennis court time.

The Failure Map — predicted before it happens
Failure #1 — The Week-3 Abandonment (probability: highest)
History says you abandon systems around week 2–3 when novelty fades and one bad day breaks the chain. Prevention: Law 06 + Reset Protocol + Minimum Days. The OS is designed so a bad day costs 2 minutes to recover from, not a restart.
Failure #2 — Outreach dies after 10 silent days
Zero replies for two weeks is STATISTICALLY NORMAL at your volume — but it will feel like proof it doesn't work. Prevention: you're graded on inputs only; the funnel card tells you 100 sent → 1 client is healthy. Re-read it on silent days.
Failure #3 — Final year eats everything (mid-Sept)
Campus 5 days/week + ExoGuard + everything else. The temptation: quietly drop outreach and gym. Prevention: Uni Day schedule pre-built with protected evening blocks; if truly overloaded, the OS drops SF first, then gym to 2×, NEVER outreach or sleep. That priority order is pre-decided — no negotiating with a tired brain.
Failure #4 — The exam that never gets booked
"Still studying" can last forever — it's avoidance wearing a study costume. Prevention: the 70×2 trigger rule. The decision is already made; your only job is logging mocks honestly.
Failure #5 — The bulk that never happens
Eating 3,000 kcal is boring and IBS makes big meals scary, so weight stays 60 kg while training hard. Prevention: liquid calories (2×/day), Friday weigh-in ritual, and the 4-week trend tile — if trend is flat for 3 weeks, add one smoothie. Mechanical, not motivational.
Failure #6 — ExoGuard perfectionism (solo builder disease)
No teammates = no one to say "that's good enough, ship it." Weeks vanish polishing CAD fillets. Prevention: Monday Contract — the supervisor meeting ships whatever exists at 80%. Deliverable streak counter makes shipping the metric, not perfection.
