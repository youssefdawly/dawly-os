Protocols — run these, don't improvise
🚨 Overwhelm Protocol
Triggered the second you notice freeze or the urge to escape into the phone.

1. Stand up. Physically. Walk to another room.
2. 3 slow breaths (4s in, 6s out) — this settles the gut too.
3. Ask ONE question: "What is the smallest next physical action?" (open the CAD file, write one sentence, send one message)
4. Do it for 10 minutes only. Permission to stop after. You almost never will.
5. Phone goes to the other room BEFORE step 4, not after.
🔄 Reset Protocol (after 2+ missed days)
The old you abandoned systems here. The new you runs this instead:

1. No guilt audit. Don't review what you missed. Delete the mental debt.
2. Declare a Minimum Day: one 30-min deep work block + 5 outreach + sleep on time. That's a full win.
3. Two Minimum Days in a row → resume normal days.
4. Write one line in the Wins Ledger: "Came back on [date]." Coming back IS the elite skill.

1. Phone in another room (non-negotiable — this is 80% of the system).

🧖 THE FRIDAY PROTOCOL
You already had the sauna and jacuzzi. Here is how to actually use them — order and timing change what you get out of it.
The sequence
  • Warm shower first — 2 min. Going into a hot room cold is uncomfortable and pointless.
  • Sauna 12–15 min. Leave when it stops being pleasant, not when a timer says so.
  • Cool down 5 min — cool shower or just sit outside. This contrast is where most of the benefit lives.
  • Repeat 2–3 rounds. Rounds beat one long brutal session every time.
  • Jacuzzi 10–15 min last — warm water, relaxed muscles, stretch gently while you are in it.
  • Drink 500ml+ water. You will have lost a real amount of fluid.
What heat actually does — and when it doesn't help
Heat increases blood flow, relaxes muscle tone and feels genuinely good, which matters more than people admit — a recovery day you look forward to is a recovery day you actually take.

What it will not do: remove lactic acid (that is gone within an hour of training anyway) or repair muscle damage. Sleep and food do that.

One real caution: avoid a long hot sauna in the 2 hours right after heavy lifting — there is decent evidence that heavy heat immediately post-training can blunt some of the adaptation. Friday is perfect precisely because it is far from your sessions.
Stretching — the 10-minute sequence
Best done warm, straight after the jacuzzi. Hold each, breathe out into it, never bounce.

Hamstrings 60s/side · hip flexor lunge 60s/side · figure-4 glutes 45s/side · chest doorway 45s · shoulder cross-body 45s/side · forearm flexor + extensor 30s each · child's pose 60s · thoracic rotations 10/side.
Napping — how to do it without wrecking the night
20 minutes, before 3 PM. That is the whole rule. Twenty minutes keeps you in light sleep so you wake sharp; 45 minutes drops you into deep sleep and you wake worse than before. After 3 PM it starts eating into your night, and your bedtime is the foundation of the whole system.

Failure #4 — The exam that never gets booked

Failure #6 — ExoGuard perfectionism (solo builder disease)
